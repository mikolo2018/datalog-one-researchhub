TEACHER / PARENT ANSWER KEY



MATHOEPEDIA                                                                                                  ANSWER KEY

Think • Model • Solve • Explain


Suggested marking: 4 marks per problem — 1 for a suitable model or operation, 1 for working, 1 for the result, and 1 for a clear
statement with units where relevant.




    1       1 Book fair change

            Books: 3 × ₦2,400 = ₦7,200. Pens: 2 × ₦350 = ₦700. Total = ₦7,900. Change = ₦10,000 − ₦7,900 =
            ₦2,100.




    2       2 Trip group

            Girls: 3/8 × 48 = 18. Boys: 48 − 18 = 30. Girls : boys = 18 : 30 = 3 : 5.




    3       3 Garden wire

            Perimeter = 2(18 + 11) = 58 m. Fence length after leaving gate = 58 − 2 = 56 m. Three rows: 3 × 56
            = 168 m of wire.




    4       4 Notebooks and rulers

            Equation: 3(₦450) + ₦200r = ₦1,750. So ₦1,350 + ₦200r = ₦1,750; ₦200r = ₦400; r = 2 rulers.




    5       5 Flour for trays

            Flour per tray = 300 ÷ 4 = 75 g. For 10 trays: 75 × 10 = 750 g. Two 500 g bags provide 1,000 g;
            leftover = 1,000 − 750 = 250 g.
